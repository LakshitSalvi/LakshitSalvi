# 🌙 Hero Module

## Purpose

The Hero is the first interaction visitors have with Project Midnight.

Its purpose is to communicate identity, professionalism, and direction in under 10 seconds.

---

## Components

1. Name
2. Professional Role
3. Brand Statement
4. Skill Tags
5. Primary Actions
6. Background Pattern

---

## Brand Statement

Building impactful solutions through data, code, and disciplined execution.

---

## Design Goals

- Premium
- Modern
- Minimal
- Technical
- Confident
- Calm

---

## Must Avoid

- Buzzwords
- Clutter
- Unnecessary animations
- Excessive colors
- Emoji-heavy design
